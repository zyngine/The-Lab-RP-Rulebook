# Zero Tolerance

Almost everything in this city gets a warning and a conversation. **These do not.** The following are permanent, first-offense bans, because they attack the server itself or the people in it.

## Cheating & exploiting → permanent ban

Any cheat software, injectors, mod menus, macros for unfair advantage, or deliberately exploiting bugs (duping money/items, out-of-map abuse, etc.).

* **First offense: permanent ban.** No warnings.
* Being caught with cheat software installed counts, whether or not you "used it here."

## Harassment → permanent ban

Real-world targeted harassment, hate speech, threats, sexual harassment, predatory behavior, doxxing, or using the server to hurt someone as a person.

* **First offense: permanent ban.** No warnings.
* Villainy **in-character** is welcome. Attacking a **real person** OOC is not, and there is no IC excuse for it.

## ERP (Erotic Roleplay) → permanent ban

The Lab does not allow erotic roleplay in any form, whether public or private, consensual or not. There is no permitted version of it here.

* **First offense: permanent ban.** No warnings.
* Romance as *story* is fine; sexual content is not. See [ERP & Content Boundaries](erp-and-content.md).

## Anything sexual involving minors → permanent ban + reported

Any sexual content involving minors or characters depicted as minors, real or fictional, in any form.

* **Zero tolerance. Permanent ban, and reported to the appropriate authorities.**
* There is no roleplay justification, ever. "It's just a character" is not a defense.

## Returning after a permanent ban

Some permanent bans are eligible for return through our **official process** only, never DMs or side deals. Cheating, harassment, ERP, and anything involving minors are reviewed individually, and some are simply **final**, with no return.

> There is no "I didn't know." These lines, **cheating, exploiting, harassment, OOC toxicity, ERP, and anything sexual involving minors**, are what keep the city safe and fair. Cross them and you're gone.
