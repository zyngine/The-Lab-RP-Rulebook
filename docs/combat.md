# Combat: RDM, VDM & Initiation

Combat is fine. **Combat without roleplay is not.** Every fight should come from a story, not appear out of nowhere.

## Initiation

Before you can shoot, rob, or attack someone, there must be **initiation**, a clear in-character interaction that gives them a real chance to respond. A demand, a threat, a reason. "I felt like it" is not initiation.

* Give people a genuine moment to comply or react. Don't demand hands up and open fire in the same second.
* Initiating on a whole group means the group can respond, but uninvolved bystanders are not automatically targets.

## RDM (Random Deathmatch)

Killing or attacking someone **without valid initiation or an RP reason** is RDM. Don't do it. A shared story, a prior conflict, an active robbery. Those are reasons. Boredom is not.

## VDM (Vehicle Deathmatch)

Using your vehicle as a weapon without RP, running people over or ramming for no reason, is VDM. Your car is a car, not a missile.

## Combat logging

**Logging out during or right after an active RP scene** (a fight, chase, robbery, or being detained) to escape it is prohibited. If you actually crash, get back as soon as you can and let people know what happened.

> **Quick test before you pull the trigger:** *Would this make sense in a story someone else is also enjoying?* If yes, go. If it only makes sense as "I wanted the kill," stop.
