# ERP & Content Boundaries

**The Lab does not permit erotic roleplay (ERP) of any kind.** This is a full, no-exceptions policy. There is no "private," "consensual," or "fade to black" version that is allowed here. Keep sexual content out of the city entirely.

## What this means

* No sexual or erotic roleplay, in public **or** private, spoken, typed, or emoted.
* No sexual content in RP, in-city channels, or anywhere the server touches.
* Romance in RP is fine as a *story*. The moment it turns sexual, it stops.

## Why it's absolute

We'd rather have one clear line everyone understands than a gray zone people "accidentally" wander into. There is nothing to interpret and nothing to negotiate: **don't bring sexual content into The Lab.**

## The hardest line of all

Any sexual content involving **minors or characters depicted as minors**, real or fictional, in any form, results in an **immediate permanent ban and a report to the appropriate authorities.** There is no roleplay justification, ever. "It's just a character" is not a defense.

> ERP is a permanent-ban offense. See [Zero Tolerance](zero-tolerance.md) for enforcement.
