# The Golden Rules

These are the core expectations. Everything else in this book is detail.

## 1. Value your life *(this one is enforced)*

Your character wants to live. When a gun is in your face, when you're outnumbered, when a situation is truly dangerous, **act like it.** Comply, negotiate, be scared, look for a real way out. Don't do things a real person facing death would never do just because "it's a game."

> **Fear RP is not optional.** Ignoring a credible threat to your life (pulling a weapon while surrounded and at gunpoint, or mouthing off to armed robbers with no plan) is one of the few RP rules we enforce firmly, because it wrecks the scene for everyone involved.

## 2. Keep IC and OOC separate

In-character conflict stays in-character. Don't carry story grudges into OOC, and don't let OOC feelings bleed into your RP. A quick OOC slip in city is no big deal. Living your OOC drama *through* your character is.

## 3. Roleplay in good faith

Give others a scene worth being in. Don't grief, don't ruin RP for the sake of "winning," and don't treat other players as NPCs. Everyone here is a person trying to tell a story, including you.

## 4. Respect people OOC, always

You can be a villain in the city and a decent person out of it. Treat other players and staff with basic respect at all times. (See [Zero Tolerance](zero-tolerance.md). This one has teeth.)
