# Reporting & Staff

## If something goes wrong in-city

* Use the in-game report system to reach staff. It's the fastest, most trackable way.
* **Don't break your current RP** to argue OOC. Finish the scene, then report; staff can review what happened.
* Gather what you can: **who** (names / IDs), **when**, and **what** happened. Clips help enormously.

## Report in good faith

Reports are for genuine rule-breaks, not for losing a fight. Filing false or spam reports to harass someone is itself a violation.

## Staff conduct

Our staff are held to a higher standard, not a looser one. They enforce with **context and fairness**, coach before they punish (outside the zero-tolerance list), and don't play favorites. If you feel a decision was wrong, there's a calm, proper channel to appeal it. Use it, rather than taking it to public drama.

## Appeals

Ban appeals and unban requests go through the **official process only**. Don't DM staff to appeal. It won't be handled there, and it may hurt your case.
