# How We Handle Rules

We don't run this city with a stopwatch and a ban hammer. **Context and intent matter.** Most rule-breaks are honest mistakes, and we treat them that way.

## Most situations

For ordinary RP mistakes like a bad initiation, breaking character, or a messy scene, staff lean toward **coaching and warnings first**, and only escalate if it keeps happening or was clearly deliberate. A first honest mistake is a conversation, not a punishment.

> Repeatedly "forgetting" the same rule stops looking like a mistake. Patterns escalate faster than one-offs.

## Some things are different

A short list of violations skips the warning ladder entirely, because they attack the integrity of the server or the safety of our players. These are **permanent, first-offense bans**, with no warnings:

* **Cheating / exploiting**
* **Harassment / OOC toxicity toward a real person**
* **ERP (erotic roleplay) of any kind**
* **Anything sexual involving minors**

See [Zero Tolerance](zero-tolerance.md) for the full list, and [Ban Guidelines](ban-guidelines.md) for exactly how everything else escalates.

## The spirit of it

If you're playing in good faith, you have a lot of room. If you're here to cheat, grief, or hurt people, you don't. Staff decisions are made case-by-case with that spirit in mind. The goal is a healthy city, not a body count.
