# Metagaming & Powergaming

Two of the most common ways good RP gets quietly ruined. Know the difference between them.

## Metagaming

Using **information your character didn't learn in-character** to affect your RP. If you know it from a stream, a Discord message, a DM, or from spectating, your character does not know it.

* Acting on something you saw on someone's **stream** ("stream sniping").
* Using **OOC info** (Discord, DMs, party chat) to find or act against someone in city.
* Knowing a stranger's name, job, or history your character was never actually told.

> Your character only knows what *they* have seen and heard in the city. That's it.

## Powergaming

Forcing outcomes, or giving your character abilities and knowledge they realistically shouldn't have, which removes other players' ability to respond.

* **Forcing actions** on someone without giving them a chance to react (`/me knocks you out and ties you up` with no opening for them to respond).
* Doing things your character couldn't realistically do, like surviving impossible injuries, having skills you never established, or fixing a car with a snap of your fingers.
* Using RP commands to *narrate reality onto* another player instead of *attempting* an action they can react to.

## The fix for both

Roleplay **attempts, not results.** Give people room to respond. Keep OOC knowledge out of IC decisions. When in doubt, ask: *does the other person still get a say in what happens?* If the answer is no, you're probably powergaming.
