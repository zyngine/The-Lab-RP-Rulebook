# Death & the New Life Rule (NLR)

How death works, and what your character is allowed to remember.

## The New Life Rule

When your character is downed and respawns (a "new life"), they **do not remember the events that led to their death**: not who killed them, why, or the details of that situation. That memory is gone.

* **Don't return** to the scene of your death while it's still active.
* **Don't seek revenge** on the people involved, based on knowledge your character shouldn't have.
* **Don't rejoin** the fight you just died in.

## If you're revived instead of respawning

If EMS or a player revives you, with no respawn and no new life, NLR generally does **not** wipe your memory. You were brought back, not reborn. Follow medical RP and any injuries or amnesia your revive scene establishes.

## Permadeath / character kills

Your character is not permanently dead from an ordinary downing. A true character kill only happens by agreement or through specific approved RP. It is not something one side can force in a random shootout.

> **The point of NLR:** death should carry weight and let a scene *end*. Using post-death knowledge to grief the people who beat you drains all the tension out of the city. Take the L, start the next story.
